# BHBC — from 3/10 to 9.5/10

Ohad, 2026-08-28: *"spend more time planning all the tools and ui and interface
and everything on bhbc, we're at 3/10 lets get to 9.5/10 +++"*

---

## 1. The reason it is 3/10 is not polish. It models the wrong thing.

He told me what the job actually is, in three messages:

> *"when i plan weights i don't need to log anything to bhbc, i just need to log
> the team sessions, which are updated in order for my planning and for the
> basketball and head coaches to see"*
>
> *"i obviusly still needs the weight sessions if theyre team weight room
> sessions to be always on the calander but i don't need an option of planning
> it"*
>
> *"just the s&c period on the court which is in the begging of the basketball
> practice and thats what i plan log and tell the basketball coaches and head
> coach about"*

So the atomic unit of this product is **the S&C period at the start of a
basketball practice**. One thing. He plans it, he runs it, he logs it, and it is
read by people who are not him.

The zone currently models something else: a generic session store where
`Practice`, `Lift` and `Game` are equal citizens, each with a planner, each with
a logger, each with its own slot maths. That symmetry is invented — it is why
there are two ways to log, three date defaults, a plan keyed differently from a
fixture, and a Lift type that can inject phantom load. **Most of the distance
from 3 to 9.5 is deleting the parts that model a job nobody does.**

### The corrected model

| Thing | Planned? | Logged? | On the calendar? | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S&C period** (start of practice) | **yes — the only thing with a planner** | yes: minutes, RPE, who was in | yes | Ohad, basketball coaches, head coach |
| Team weight-room session | **no planner** | attendance + minutes only, no RPE | **yes, always** | Ohad |
| Basketball practice (the rest of it) | no | no | yes, as context | context only |
| Game | no | no | yes | everyone |

Two consequences fall straight out:

- **`Lift` stops being a loggable session type with an RPE field.** It is a
  calendar fact plus attendance. That deletes the whole class of bug I fixed
  today (stale RPE injecting 420 AU into a gym session) rather than guarding it.
- **The planner has exactly one subject.** Not "a session" — *the S&C period*.
  Its fields are the ones he actually briefs: focus, the block, the constraint
  (who is limited/out), and the duration.

## 2. What the zone is FOR — one sentence per screen

Right now every tab is a container of cards. A screen should answer one question.

| Tab | The question it answers | Today |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | *What do I do today, and who can't do it?* | six cards, three of which repeat each other |
| **Schedule** | *What is the week, and what is my S&C period in each?* | month/week/list toggle + a separate planner |
| **Roster** | *Who is on the squad and how loaded are they?* | a 700px table |
| **Medical** | *Who is hurt, what can they do, when are they back?* | correct, and the best screen in the zone |
| **Games** | *What is coming, how did we do?* | mixes league-wide data with BHBC's own |

**Sessions** should not be a tab. It is the S&C runner and belongs behind the
"start" action on Overview.

## 3. The redesign, screen by screen

### 3.1 Overview — one answer, not six cards

Collapse to three regions, in this order:

1. **TODAY** — the S&C period(s), each as a single line the coach can act on:
   `16:00 · S&C · 20 min · focus: landing mechanics · 12 available, 2 limited`
   with one primary action (`Start` if it is now, `Plan` if it is not yet
   written, `Log` if it has been run). One row, one verb.
2. **WHO CANNOT DO IT** — the limited/out list with the reason and the return
   date. This is the thing he tells the basketball coach, so it must be
   copyable as text in one tap.
3. **LOAD** — the board, but read-only here: name, ACWR band, 7-day. The
   editing lives in Roster.

Delete: the S&C Brief's overlap with Today, the duplicate Next Game panel
(it belongs on Games with a one-line echo here), and the separate "availability"
counters that repeat region 2.

### 3.2 The S&C period planner — the one real authoring surface

Today the planner is a week grid where a "session" is type + time + minutes +
free text. It should be **a card per S&C period** with the fields he briefs:

- **Focus** (one line, the thing he says out loud)
- **Block / phase** — pulled from the microcycle (MD-3 etc.), not retyped
- **Duration**
- **Constraints** — auto-filled from Medical: who is limited, non-contact, out.
  He should never retype what the medical board already knows.
- **Notes for the basketball staff** — because that is the actual output.

And it must produce **the coach-facing artefact**: a short, copyable summary of
today's S&C period for the head coach. That artefact is the product's whole
reason to exist and it does not exist today.

### 3.3 Schedule — calendar as a read surface

- **Month view on a phone falls back to List.** At 390px, seven columns give
  ~29px of content per day; the current fix (`min-width:0`) removed the
  overflow by shredding the content.
- Weight-room sessions render as a **calendar fact with no planner affordance**
  — a muted chip, no `+ plan this session`.
- The week planner's inline add/edit row (currently ~6 stacked lines on a phone)
  becomes a sheet, not an inline row.

### 3.4 Roster — a phone-first list, not a 700px grid

`rowGrid` has a hard floor of 700px, so at 390px the two controls a coach
actually taps — cycle availability and report an injury — sit off-screen at
x≈350 and x≈608. Restructure per row:

```
#12  עמית מנחם                    [ ACWR pill ]
     limited · ankle · RTP 26 Aug   [availability] [+ MED]
```

Two lines, both actions in the thumb zone, the sparkline and 28-day figures
behind a tap. Horizontal scroll is right for the standings table; it is wrong
for the row you operate.

### 3.5 Medical — keep, and give it attribution

The strongest screen already. One addition he asked for: **who wrote this**.
Every injury record and every rehab note gets `by <name> · <date>`, and the
practice log records who logged it. Two PTs share this board now; an unsigned
medical record is a liability.

## 4. Tools — what a coach can actually do

Current tool surface is: log a session, log a practice, plan a session, report
an injury, edit a game, manage roster. Six modals, three of which overlap.

The set should be:

1. **Plan the S&C period** (the authoring surface above)
2. **Run it** — the live runner, from Overview
3. **Log it** — minutes + RPE + who was in; one screen, phone-first
4. **Report / update an injury** — as today, plus attribution
5. **Brief the staff** — generate the copyable summary

Delete: the separate "log a session" vs "log practice" duality, the check-in
flow (already removed), and planning affordances on weight-room sessions.

---

## 5. Mobile — the floor is the primary device

Measured today at 390px:

- Sticky header eats **~149px, 18% of the viewport**, in three stacked rows, and
  the GAMES tab is off-screen with no scroll affordance.
- Practice-entry modal's field row needs **~460px inside 310px** — and it holds
  Minutes and RPE, the two fields the save requires.
- LoadBoard is a **700px** grid; both write actions are off-screen.
- `✕` delete buttons are **~24×16px, 4px from `✎`**, with no confirm and no undo.

Rules for the pass:
- Any control that WRITES must be reachable without horizontal scrolling.
- Destructive actions get a confirm, or get separated from their neighbour, or
  get undo. Currently they have none of the three.
- Touch targets ≥ 40px in both dimensions where they sit adjacent to another
  control.
- Header collapses to one row on scroll.

## 6. Liveness — already solved, and over-solved

The audit's finding, which inverts the ask: BHBC is **already the most live zone
in the app** — every store key has a per-key realtime subscription, plus a
second `bhbc-live` channel, plus a 5s poll. The programs screen he is comparing
it to has *none* of that (`plans` is not even in the realtime publication).

The real gaps are narrow:

1. **Gym minutes and bodyweight are not live at all** (`client_workouts`,
   `bw_logs` — no subscription, not in the publication).
2. **An open modal holds a stale snapshot and writes it back.** If Tomer
   resolves an injury while Ohad has that injury open, Ohad's save silently
   reverts it. The plans editor already solved this with a non-destructive
   "remote edit" banner — copy that, do not auto-apply under an open editor.
3. `expo-bhbc-roster` is regenerated **only in the owner's browser**, so a staff
   edit never reaches the coaches until Ohad opens the app.

Do **not** add a fourth refresh mechanism. Delete the App.jsx duplicate layer
and give `useSupaStore`'s per-key subscription the 5s fallback, so all six keys
behave identically.

## 7. Order of work

**First — correctness the coach can feel** (mostly done today): phantom load,
monotony inversion, past games as upcoming, the injury floor, double-counted
practices, the backdated-save data loss.

**Second — the model.** Make the S&C period the unit: one planner, one logger,
weight-room sessions as calendar facts. This is the change that moves the score.

**Third — the artefact.** The copyable brief for the basketball/head coach.
Nothing else in the zone produces the thing he says he needs.

**Fourth — mobile.** Roster row, practice-entry modal, header budget,
destructive actions.

**Fifth — attribution + the stale-modal guard.** Both are two-coach problems and
both are now live problems.

**Sixth — polish.** Type scale, focus rings, empty states, motion.

## 8. What I will not do without a decision from him

- Collapsing **Sessions** out of the tab bar (it is a real surface today).
- Removing the `Lift` logging path outright — it currently records gym
  attendance, and attendance may be the point even if load is not.
- Changing the roster row layout, which is the screen he looks at most.
